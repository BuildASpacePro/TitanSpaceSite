New Beginnings

Today marks the beginning of Titan Space. We're building the next generation of satellites with a focus on mass production, vertical integration, and we'll do this using AI-first engineering workflows.

So why us and not our competitors? Why does this have to happen? Launch costs are at an all time low and yet no satellite manufacturer is taking full advantage of this. Satellites are still designed in the same way they were 20 years ago - bespoke, designed by hand and built in small numbers.

Our mission is clear: to design satellites for mass production and produce them faster and cheaper than anyone else. This needs to happen so the space ecosystem in the UK and Europe can flourish. What advantages do we have over industry incumbents?

  • Satellite design - ours uses less parts, takes less time to assemble and costs less to build and test
  • AI/LLM integration - enables engineers to work faster than ever before
  • Vertical integration - full control over the supply chain and can iterate faster than anyone else
  • Rust software stack - offers memory safety, reduces vulnerabilities and allows us to build a more robust and secure applications
  • Starship - nobody else in Europe is designing satellites for deployment from Starship, which will be a game changer for the industry. We are and will be the best at doing this

If you want to join us on this exciting mission and build the future of the UK space industry, check out our careers page now.
